## Status vocabulary

Use a small vocabulary:

- **Proven** — direct evidence is sufficient for this decision.
- **Partly proven** — evidence supports only part of the claim.
- **Inferred** — plausible conclusion from indirect evidence.
- **Unknown** — material evidence is absent.
- **Contradicted** — available evidence conflicts with the claim.

## Review rule

A high-impact claim left as unknown or inferred should be visible in the final decision record. Do not make uncertainty disappear by converting it into a numerical score.
