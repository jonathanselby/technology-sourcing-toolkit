# SaaS Renewal Review

## Purpose

A SaaS renewal is a fresh sourcing decision with better evidence than the original purchase.

By renewal time the organisation should know far more about actual usage, integration effort, supplier behaviour, support quality, workarounds, realised cost and switching difficulty. Renewal should therefore be evidence-led rather than an administrative continuation.

## Review the evidence

### Usage

- active users versus licensed users;
- feature usage;
- peak and average transaction volumes;
- business-critical workflows now dependent on the product;
- unused modules or licences.

### Operating evidence

- incidents and outages;
- support responsiveness;
- internal administration effort;
- integration maintenance;
- manual workarounds;
- release impacts;
- control or audit effort.

### Commercial evidence

- original forecast versus realised spend;
- unit-cost movement;
- growth assumptions;
- support or environment charges;
- contractual changes;
- renewal uplift;
- termination and transition obligations.

### Strategic evidence

- roadmap commitments delivered or missed;
- new viable alternatives;
- business dependence created since purchase;
- data/configuration portability;
- skills and knowledge concentration.

## Renewal options

Do not reduce the decision to renew versus cancel.

Possible choices include:

```text
RENEW AS IS
RENEW + RIGHTSIZING
RENEGOTIATE STRUCTURE
REDUCE SCOPE
DUAL-RUN / TRANSITION
REPLACE
INTERNALISE A BOUNDED CAPABILITY
DEFER DECISION WITH EXPLICIT CONDITIONS
```

## Renewal decision record

Record:

- what changed since the original decision;
- which original assumptions proved right or wrong;
- current dependency;
- realistic alternatives;
- exit cost now;
- expected cost over the next term;
- conditions for another renewal.

## Core rule

> **Do not renew the sales story. Renew the operating evidence.**
